Ch. 01 The throughline

Research that ships.

My work started with a question in a bioinformatics lab: could machine learning actually change outcomes in medicine? That curiosity turned into published research, a Best Paper Award in computer vision, and a privacy-preserving model that predicts ICU mortality without ever moving a patient's data.

But research only matters when it runs. So I learned to ship — building agentic LLM systems inside Siemens' industrial software, designing the serving stacks and evaluation loops that prove a model works under real load. Today, at Carnegie Mellon and Qualcomm, I live in that seam between the frontier and production.

  • From 0 → 1 I prototype fast, then harden into reliable, observable systems.
  • Evidence-driven Offline + online evaluation, A/B tests, drift dashboards.
  • High-stakes domains Especially healthcare, where correctness and trust matter.
